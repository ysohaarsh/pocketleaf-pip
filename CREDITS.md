# Credits

## Assets

All art, animation, audio, music and the bitmap font in Pip & the Pocket Kingdom are **generated in
code by this project** (sprite and font data in `src/engine/`, synth and songs in `src/audio/`).

**No third-party assets are used.** Any future third-party asset must be CC0 and listed here with its
source and license.
